Privacy Policy

Metravuno · Last updated November 11, 2026

Who we are

Metravuno publishes news explainer videos on its own Facebook page and YouTube channel. "Metravuno Replies" is an internal tool used only by the Metravuno channel owner.

What the tool does

The tool uses the official YouTube Data API and Facebook Graph API to read public comments posted on Metravuno's own videos and posts, and to reply to them on Metravuno's behalf. It is not offered to other users and does not access any other channel, page or account.

Data we access

Only public comments on Metravuno's own content and the basic public information the platforms attach to them (such as comment text, time and display name).

How we use and store it

Comments are used only to decide whether and how to reply. We keep a short log of replies made, for quality review. We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties, do not use it for advertising, and do not build profiles of commenters.

Google API Services

Metravuno Replies' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deletion

To ask us to delete a logged reply involving your comment, message the Metravuno Facebook page. You can also delete your own comments at any time on the platform where you posted them.

Contact

Message us on our Facebook page.
